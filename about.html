About

Empowering community and socio-economic resilience — Bridge to GiveBack.

Empowered Bridge Foundation is a youth-led NGO founded in 2023 and headquartered in Huye, Southern Province, with work in Kigali and Kirehe. We were known as Youth Impact Hub until 2026, when we took a name that matches the work: building bridges between potential and purpose.

Our conviction is simple. Socio-economic resilience begins with inclusive education. Children from vulnerable families, street-connected youth, women, and people with disabilities are too often left outside the systems that should serve them. We meet that gap with practical programs — meals, school kits, mentorship, sports, arts, and advocacy — so young people become impact makers who solve community challenges themselves.

Mission

Break barriers. Build makers.

Equip youth to transform ideas into solutions through education, capacity building, peace building, and inclusion — supporting children from vulnerable families along the way.

Vision

Communities where no one is left behind.

A Rwanda where young people, women, and people with disabilities are celebrated for their capability, not defined by isolation or exclusion.

How we work

A practical hub, not a distant office

Each one, teach one

Peer mentorship sits inside our programs — from 30-minute discussions after sports to scholar visits at GS Gatore.

Learn through doing

Arts, entertainment, and sport are not extras. They are how we teach, gather, and restore dignity.

Partnership first

Collaboration is open. We work with families, schools, centres, volunteers, and institutions who share the work on the ground.
