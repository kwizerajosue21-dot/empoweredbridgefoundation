Sangira n’Umwana ku Ishuri

Sharing lunch with children at school

Hunger should not decide whether a child stays in school. This flagship, youth-led initiative restores meals, materials, and dignity. Last year we fed and equipped 100 children. We are scaling toward 500 lunches and 100 full school kits.

  • RWF 1,000 provides one meal
  • RWF 20,000 provides one full school kit
  • In-kind gifts of food, hygiene, and supplies are welcome

Kirehe District

Sports for development

Basketball, taekwondo, and peer mentorship

In rural communities, sport is a vehicle for social integration and youth mobilization. After training, young people sit for 30 minutes of peer-led conversation. The aims are clear:

  • Return street-connected children to formal education
  • Rebuild support between vulnerable youth and their parents
  • Identify grassroots athletic talent
  • Offer structured, daily mentorship

Gicumbi District

Disability inclusion

Centre Nyinawimana Izere

True community development cannot happen without total inclusion. We work with Centre Nyinawimana to understand real-time challenges faced by children with disabilities in rural areas, strengthen advocacy, and design adaptive paths to education and economic opportunity.

On 16 September 2026 we host a Disability Inclusion Awareness initiative and are raising 1,000,000 FRW for specialized learning aids, notebooks, and backpacks.
