Who we are

Youth turning ideas into solutions that communities can feel.

We are a youth-led NGO focused on capacity building, education promotion, peace building, and inclusivity for women and people with disabilities. We support children from vulnerable families and learn by doing — through mentorship, arts, sports, and practical service.

In 2026, Youth Impact Hub became Empowered Bridge Foundation: the same community, a wider bridge.

01

Education promotion

Mentorship, skills, and a practical hub where young people turn ideas into community solutions.

03

Family reconciliation, peer dialogue, and sports that reconnect street-connected youth with support systems.

04

Inclusion

Women and people with disabilities at the center — from pity to celebration of unique capability.
